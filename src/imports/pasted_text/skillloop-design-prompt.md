# 1. PRODUCT CONCEPT

Design a modern, premium, responsive web application called **SkillLoop**.

SkillLoop is a local economic opportunity platform that connects:

1. Students and skilled young people who want to earn income.
2. Students, lecturers, university organizations, small businesses, and nearby community members who need affordable services.

SkillLoop is NOT a generic freelancing marketplace.

Its key differentiator is:

### AI-Powered Local Opportunity Discovery

Instead of only waiting for users to post jobs, SkillLoop identifies patterns in local requests and surfaces hidden demand.

Example:

Several people post:

* "Need an event poster."
* "Looking for someone to design a flyer."
* "Need social media graphics."

SkillLoop detects:

**Graphic Design Demand Detected**

and creates an opportunity for suitable students.

The product should communicate:

**Discover → Match → Earn → Grow**
